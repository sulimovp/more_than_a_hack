How the official labels count possession (read once)
  • A team gets the ball with its first controlled action: a pass, a dribble that keeps the ball, a trap. A block, a deflection, a header that just clears, or a tackle that loses the ball again at once does not change possession.
  • A loose ball and a ball in flight still belong to the team that last controlled it.
  • Play is dead from the moment the ball leaves the pitch or the referee stops play, until the restart is taken (the throw, the kick). Who restarts it is the team in possession from that moment.
  • The orange line is when our system thinks the change happens. "Yes, here" means within about 1 second of it. If the real moment is further away, pause there and use button 2.
  • The broadcast picture sometimes shows a replay or a close-up of something else; trust the tactical view then.
