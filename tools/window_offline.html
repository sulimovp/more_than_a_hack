How the official labels count possession (read once)
  • A team gets the ball with its first controlled action: a pass, a dribble that keeps the ball, a trap. A block, a deflection, a header that just clears, or a tackle that loses the ball again at once does not change possession.
  • A loose ball and a ball in flight still belong to the team that last controlled it.
  • Dead from the moment the ball leaves the pitch or the referee stops play, until the restart is taken (the throw, the kick). The team taking the restart has the ball from that moment.
  • Mark the label at the frame the change happens; pause and step frames to get it within about half a second. A mark can be removed by clicking it.
